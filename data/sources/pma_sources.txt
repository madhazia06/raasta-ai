# PMA (Punjab Mass Transit Authority) Sources
# Used for Metro (Lahore Metrobus) and Speedo route/fare/timing data

*** PRIMARY / AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE (used for all 34 Speedo route stop lists,
distances, and vehicle class in routes.csv) ***
Official PMA integrated public transport network plan (PDF, all 122 planned
routes across Phase I-VIII, with route length, speed, headway, bus count):
https://pma.punjab.gov.pk/system/files?file=Lahore-+Public+Transport+Routes_1.pdf

IMPORTANT FINDING: This document confirms "Speedo" buses ARE Lahore Metrobus's
feeder network. PMA's internal name is "Feeder Route N"; the public-facing
brand is "Speedo". Only Phase I (routes 1-14) and Phase II (routes 15-34) are
currently operating -- Phase III-VIII (routes 35-122) are future/planned and
were excluded from this dataset since they don't exist as active service yet.

Official PMA feeder route directory (per-route pages, confirms interchange
points with Metro/Orange Line):
https://pma.punjab.gov.pk/lahore-feeder-route-phaseI

Official PMA site (Lahore Metrobus System):
https://pma.punjab.gov.pk/lahore_metrobus_system

Wikipedia - Lahore Metrobus (overview, history, ridership):
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_Metrobus

Wikipedia - Niazi Chowk Metrobus Station (station-level detail/coords):
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niazi_Chowk_Metrobus_Station

Wikipedia - Timber Market Metrobus Station (station-level detail/coords):
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timber_Market_Metrobus_Station

Metro Bus Lahore - full 27-station list, feeder routes, fares, timings (raabty.com):
https://raabty.com/blog/metro-bus-lahore

Metro Bus Routes Lahore - fares, history, timings (graana.com):
https://www.graana.com/blog/metro-bus-routes-lahore-ticket-price-timings-more/

Speedo Bus Routes in Lahore - 34 route list + detailed stop sequences (icons.com.pk):
https://icons.com.pk/speedo-bus-routes-lahore

Speedo Bus Lahore Routes and Timing (pakinfodesk.pk):
https://pakinfodesk.pk/speedo-bus-lahore-routes-and-timing/
